EAP-TLS FIELD CHECKLIST

Authentication needs context.

No client certificate or expired certificate

Check the selected certificate, private-key access, authentication profile, validity dates, and endpoint clock. Match the failed attempt to server and supplicant logs.

Trust chain, identity, or purpose mismatch

Check the intended root CA and intermediates on the validating side. Confirm the expected server identity and the certificate's intended usage. A valid signature alone is not enough.

Revocation or TLS negotiation failure

Separate an actually revoked certificate from an unreachable revocation service. Check policy and logs. For negotiation errors, compare supported TLS versions and cipher suites.

Large exchanges stall over a VPN

Compare captures at both tunnel ends, usable MTU, ICMP messages, and missing fragments. TCP MSS does not resize UDP RADIUS traffic.
