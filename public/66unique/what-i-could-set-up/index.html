The gap

02Two things I noticed

The first one: none of it connects to anything

Every one of those 137,000 people hits the same wall if they get curious. The only link on your profiles goes to the Weebly, and the Weebly has no stock, no prices, and no way to say "hold that for me."

So somebody watches a 629,000 view video, thinks "I want that," and there is nowhere to go. They forget by dinner.

You have a national audience for the knowledge and a local audience for the shop, and right now the website serves neither one.

The second one: YouTube is the only channel falling out

You are already putting the same video on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, and they all go out together. That habit is the hard part and you have it. YouTube is the one that gets missed.

Part of the build

A "Just In" page

Ten or twenty things a week. Photo, one line, booth number, price, and your phone number to call and hold it. No shopping cart, because your stock is one of one and it sells off the floor anyway.

Posting an item takes about thirty seconds on your phone, standing in front of it. If it took longer it would never get used, so that is the whole design. Sold items stay up and go grey, because that is how people know things move.

Consulting

One post, all four channels, YouTube included

You already put the same video on three of them. This makes it one action instead of three, adds YouTube to the list, and lets you queue things ahead instead of posting live. I run this exact tool for my own accounts.

Worth knowing up front: connecting to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube properly means registering an app with each of them and waiting on their approval. That part is out of my hands and can take a couple of weeks. I do the paperwork and the chasing, and we only sit down together once the approvals land.

Consulting

Short and practical, not a binder. Which of your formats belongs on which channel, how often, what time of day, and what to do with the ones that do well. Your recurring bits like the hot items to watch and Collecting 101 become slots you fill instead of things you think up on the spot.

Includes a one page card per format so anyone helping you knows what good looks like.
