What's inside

Every one of these photographs was taken inside the shop. The stock turns over constantly, so treat this as the flavor rather than the inventory. Regulars say they see something new every visit.

Also on the floor: clocks, toys, wicker, dolls, coins, comics, cast iron cookware, fishing gear, architectural pieces, vintage train sets, Coca-Cola and Pepsi, and the Pinterest project stock people come in specifically to hunt for.

More than a hundred vendors already sell here. You pick the size, you keep every dollar your booth takes in, and there is no storefront overhead hanging over you.

Sell your goods without the risk of high overhead from owning your own store front. Any size space you choose. All monthly sales from your booth come straight back to you, by check, on the 1st of every month.

We buy, and we pick

Not everybody wants a booth. We also buy antiques and collectibles outright. Bring the item in, or call us and we will come out to you, take a look at what you have, and offer a reasonable price.

We also like to do some American Picking. If you have an old barn or a house full of old items, give us a call and we will come out, pick through it, and make you an offer on the lot.

Appraisals too. Want to know what something is worth? Bring it in or call with the details and we will do the research and tell you. Fees can apply.
